Mission

Purpose is the
antidote to burnout

Ember empowers patients to write recognition letters and share their care outcomes directly with the nurses, physicians, and support staff who made a difference. By surfacing genuine patient gratitude in real time, Ember restores a sense of purpose to healthcare workers — the one thing no HR policy can manufacture.

We partner with hospitals to deliver a HIPAA-compliant, mobile-first platform that integrates with Epic, Cerner, and Medtech — making recognition effortless for patients and meaningful for staff.
